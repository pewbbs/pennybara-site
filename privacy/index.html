Privacy Policy

Effective: November 19, 2026

Pennybara ("the app") is a personal record-keeping app for household money. This policy explains what happens to the information you put into it.

The short version

  • We don't collect any of your data. The app has no servers, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking.
  • Your records stay with you. They're stored on your device and, if you turn on iCloud sync, in your own private iCloud database, which only your Apple Account can access.
  • Nothing connects to your bank. The app never asks for bank passwords, PINs, one-time codes, full card numbers or CVVs.

What you enter

Accounts, balances, transactions, budgets, goals, bills, card statements, investment values, notes and optional receipt images are created by you and kept:

  1. On your device, in the app's private storage, protected by iOS data protection.
  2. In your private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit), if iCloud sync is on, so your other devices signed in to the same Apple Account can see them. Apple stores this data under its own privacy policy; the developer cannot read it.

Settings

App preferences (for example, whether Capi's tips are on) are stored on your device only.

Notifications

If you turn on reminders, the app schedules local notifications on your device. Notification text names the bill or card and the date; it never includes balances or amounts.

Face ID

If you turn on App Lock, iOS checks your face or passcode. The app only receives "unlocked" or "not unlocked"; biometric data never leaves the Secure Enclave.

Backups and sharing

Exports (full backup or CSV) are created only when you ask, and go only where you choose to share or save them.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Deleting your data

Delete records in the app, or delete the app to remove its local data. To remove synced data, turn off iCloud sync and delete the app's data in Settings › [your name] › iCloud › Manage Storage.
