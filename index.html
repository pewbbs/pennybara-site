A private money tracker for your household

For iPhone and iPad.

You enter your accounts and transactions; Pennybara turns them into a clear picture of what you own, what you owe, where the money went and what's coming due.

  • Nothing connects to your bank. The app never asks for bank passwords, PINs or card numbers.
  • Your records stay on your device and sync only through your own private iCloud.
  • No accounts, no servers, no ads, no tracking.
  • Capi the capybara shows you around.
