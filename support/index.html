Getting started

Open the app and Capi will offer a short tour. You can replay any walkthrough from the Capi button on Home, or from Settings › Capi.

Common questions

Does the app connect to my bank?
No. You enter balances and transactions yourself. The app never asks for bank passwords, PINs or card numbers.

How do I sync between my iPhone, iPad and Mac?
Sign in to the same Apple Account on each device and keep iCloud Drive on. Check Settings › iCloud Sync in the app for the current status. Your data syncs only between your own devices.

Can my family see my records?
No. Each person's data lives in their own private iCloud. Family members who install the app get their own separate records.

Sync says "needs attention".
Make sure you're signed in to iCloud and have storage space, then fully close and reopen the app.

How do I back up or move my data?
Settings › Export › Full Backup creates a file you can save anywhere; Settings › Import restores it. Receipt images aren't included in backups.

Why is a number marked "Estimate"?
Card statements are estimated from your records until you enter the bank's own figures. Your bank's statement is always the authority.
